# PichiTube v1.0.0 — Official Master Release 🚀

Welcome to the official **v1.0.0 release** of **PichiTube**, the lightweight, privacy-respecting, and 100% ad-free YouTube streaming client built exclusively for Android with Jetpack Compose & Material 3.

---

## ✨ What's New in v1.0.0

- 🛡️ **100% Ad-Free Experience:** All video ads, banners, and interruptions are permanently blocked.
- ⏭️ **Built-in SponsorBlock:** Automatically skips sponsored segments, intros, and reminders with toast alerts.
- 🎬 **Up to 4K (2160p) & 2K (1440p) Playback:** Support for Ultra HD video streams seamlessly merged with high-bitrate audio via ExoPlayer's `MergingMediaSource`.
- 🎧 **Background Audio & Media Notification:** Full MediaSession integration with lock-screen and status-bar notification controls (play/pause, seek, artwork, channel).
- ⚡ **Dedicated Shorts Player:** YouTube Shorts tab with smooth vertical swipe navigation (`VerticalPager`), channel info, and auto-playback.
- 🔍 **Smart Live Search:** Instant real-time YouTube search suggestions, matched query bolding, and insert-arrow actions.
- 🇧🇩 **Bangladesh Regional Trends & Endless Feed:** Default Bangladesh regional content kiosk with personalized watch-history-driven recommendations and non-stop infinite scrolling.
- ⏱️ **Sleep Timer with 'Stop After This Video':** Timer options including 10, 15, 30, 45, 60 minutes and "End of video".
- 🔄 **In-App Direct Self-Updater:** Download and install future updates directly within the app without needing an external browser.
- 🔒 **Zero Telemetry & 100% Privacy:** No Google account required. All watch history, subscriptions, and preferences remain strictly on your device.
